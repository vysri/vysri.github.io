<div class="bio-column" markdown="1">

Hello! I'm a PhD student at the University of Washington in the [Ubicomp Lab](https://ubicomplab.cs.washington.edu/) advised by [Prof. Shwetak Patel](https://www.cs.washington.edu/people/faculty/shwetak-patel/). I have many interests, but am currently focusing on conversational AI.

My research on conversational AI systems spans two complementary areas. One focuses on efficient model execution, including on-device inference and distributed inference across multiple devices, to enable responsive and intelligent interactions. The other investigates how conversational AI systems can elicit and gather context from users in real-world settings, where information is often incomplete, ambiguous, or requires further exploration.

I was previously at the University of Michigan ECE, where I worked with [Prof. Robert Dick](https://robertdick.org/). I also worked on computer architecture for a while, both at Michigan and at SambaNova Systems.

Please reach out at vysri [at] cs [dot] washington [dot] edu if you'd like to chat about research, interesting ideas, or anything else!
